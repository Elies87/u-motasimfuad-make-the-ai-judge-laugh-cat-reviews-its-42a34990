A one-star review written by a house cat about its human, entered in the
[make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
**"Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human"**
(`u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990`).

## The entry

> One star. He locks the bathroom door like I don't pay rent. I don't.
> Neither does he. The bank owns us both, and it has never once fed me.

138 characters, one line, no emoji — a deadpan one-star review with a sting
in the tail. The complaint is specific and instantly recognisable to any cat
owner (the locked bathroom door); the twist turns the rent argument back on
the human (he doesn't pay rent either — the bank owns them both); and the
final clause judges the bank by the only standard a cat recognises: it has
never once fed me.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the review to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). The output is fully
deterministic — every run produces byte-identical text.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
